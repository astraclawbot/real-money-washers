Money goes everywhere. We believe it deserves a moment in the tray.

A literal approach.

Real Money Washers began with a small observation: a banknote can change hands hundreds of times without anyone offering it a clean towel. Our fictional institute exists to correct this imbalance of hospitality.

We deal in the physical condition of money. The creased note. The pocket-worn coin. The collection of small change that has acquired the atmosphere of a cup holder. Each deserves careful attention, regardless of denomination.

Our ambition is modest: return every specimen looking more considered than it arrived. We cannot improve its purchasing power. We have checked.

Look before you wash.
Material, surface wear and existing damage determine the treatment. A delicate specimen earns a lighter hand, not a more impressive procedure.
Preserve its character.
We aim to remove surface dirt, not evidence of a life in circulation. A crease is not a moral failing.
Keep the account clear.
