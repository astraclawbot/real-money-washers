Do you actually wash money?

In the world of Real Money Washers, yes: physical notes and coins, with entirely literal cleaning methods. This website is a fictional creative experience; no actual currency is accepted.

Will my money be worth more?

No. A denomination is a denomination. The proposed improvement is entirely in its presentation, and our prices are illustrative CAD rates.

Can every note be treated?

Some marks should be left alone. Our fictional inspection considers condition and material before treatment. Fragile or collectable pieces belong with a specialist conservator.

What happens when I request a wash?

You choose a treatment and quantity, then receive a fictional collection receipt in your browser. There is no payment, shipping or real booking, and your form is not sent anywhere.
