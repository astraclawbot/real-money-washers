Before the tray.

Will this make old money look new?

That is beyond our fictional remit. Treatments focus on surface dirt and presentation. Wear, ink loss, tears and corrosion are not reversible promises.

How do I count a bulk order?

One batch covers up to 20 individual items. For 21 items, select two batches; for 40, select two. The form quotes by batches, not by the number of items inside them.

Do I send money or pay online?

No. This is a fictional brand experience. The request form creates a receipt in your browser and sends nothing to us. There is no delivery address, payment step or actual collection.

Can I mix treatments?

The receipt covers one selected service. Choose bulk care for a mixed collection, or create separate receipts for individual treatments.
